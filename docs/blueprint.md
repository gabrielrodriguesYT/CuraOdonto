# **App Name**: CuraOdonto

## Core Features:

- Animated Header & Navigation: Dynamic header with smooth animations, providing intuitive navigation across different sections of the landing page.
- Hero Section with Call-to-Action: An inviting initial view highlighting the clinic's main benefits and featuring a clear call-to-action for scheduling a consultation.
- Interactive Gallery & Testimonials Carousel: A single interactive carousel to elegantly display clinic images and highlight client feedback in a engaging format.
- Detailed Services Overview: A dedicated section presenting all dental services offered, each with concise and clear descriptions.
- Appointment Request Form: A user-friendly form allowing prospective patients to submit their details and preferred appointment times.
- AI-Powered Dental Info Assistant: An intelligent chatbot tool that provides instant answers to common dental questions, guiding users towards relevant services and general information.
- Contact & Location Details: Essential clinic information including address, phone number, and opening hours for easy access.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary blue (#4792C4): A serene blue, chosen to reflect trust, professionalism, and a calming environment.
- Light tinted background (#EEF5F8): A very subtle blue-white for the main background, providing a clean, airy, and modern feel.
- Mint green accent (#51CCB9): A refreshing mint green used to highlight key information, calls-to-action, and evoke feelings of health and well-being.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for a modern, objective, and highly readable appearance across all content.
- Use clean, minimalist line icons, particularly for services and contact information, to maintain a sleek and professional modern aesthetic.
- Clean, responsive, and full-width layout with clearly delineated sections, ensuring optimal readability and ease of navigation on all devices.
- Subtle and smooth transition animations for header elements, section scrolling, and interactive carousel slides to enhance user engagement and provide a dynamic experience.
